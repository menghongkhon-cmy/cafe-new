The Journey of HONG Coffee

Founded in Phnom Penh, HONG Coffee was created to provide premium quality coffee in a warm, cozy, and professional environment. Whether you are looking for a tranquil space to study, a creative hub to work, or a comfortable corner to catch up with friends, our doors are always open.

We believe that great coffee starts long before it reaches your cup—from sustainably sourced highland beans to meticulous roasting profiles and master craftsmanship.
